Privacy Policy

for the iOS app “Prog - Weight Progression” (“Prog”) · Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: Prog stores all your data exclusively on your device. There is no user account, no server, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools. The developer has no access to your photos, weight data or any other data. Purchases of Prog Pro are handled entirely by Apple.

2. Data processed by the app

When you use Prog, the app processes the following data:

  • photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library
  • your weight and the date of your entries
  • your goal weight and app settings (e.g. app lock, showing the previous photo)
  • optionally: weight values from or for Apple Health (see section 3)

This data is stored exclusively in the app’s protected storage on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Photos and weight data may allow conclusions about your health. They are processed solely on your device and under your control.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Only to take photos for your entries, and only when you open the camera in the app yourself.
  • Photo library: Photos are selected through the iOS system picker. The app only receives the one photo you choose and has no access to the rest of your library. Prog only writes to your photo library when you save a timelapse video or comparison image via the share menu.
  • Apple Health: Optional (Prog Pro). If you turn on syncing, Prog saves the weight you enter to Apple Health and can read your most recent weight from Health, e.g. from a smart scale. Health data is not transmitted to the developer or third parties and is not used for advertising or any other purpose. You decide in the Health app which data Prog may read and write.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: Optional, to lock the app. Authentication is handled by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded. Biometric data is not accessible to the app and never leaves your device.

You can revoke permissions at any time in the iOS Settings.

4. Backups

In-app backup: Only when you start it yourself, Prog creates a backup file containing your photos, weight data and goal weight. You decide where the file is stored (e.g. on your device, in iCloud Drive or on your computer). The file is not encrypted. If you store it with a cloud provider, that provider’s privacy policy applies.

Device backup: Depending on your iOS settings, Prog’s data may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your device. This is controlled by Apple and by you, not by the app.

5. App Store and Apple

The app is downloaded and updated through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes data under its own responsibility; see Apple’s Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase: Prog Pro is sold as a one-time purchase through Apple. Payment, Apple ID and billing are processed by Apple. The app only receives a purchase confirmation signed by Apple, which is verified directly on your device. The developer receives no payment or personal data, only anonymized sales figures.

If you have agreed in the iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides the developer with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. The developer cannot link this data to any person. You can withdraw your consent at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Contact by email

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the content of your message are processed to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted once your request has been resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries in the app by swiping left in the list. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Backup files you have saved yourself must be deleted by you if needed.

If Apple Health syncing is on, deleting an entry also removes the corresponding Health value. Values already stored in Health remain there after the app is deleted and can be deleted in the Health app.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Since the developer does not receive any personal data from you through the app, there is generally no data to which these rights could apply. You can contact the address above with any questions at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The competent authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA) in Ansbach, Germany.

9. Changes

This privacy policy will be updated when the app or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page. In case of doubt, the German version prevails.
